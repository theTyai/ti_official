ABOUT THE CHAPTER

Think. Connect. Impact.

Creating a platform where students can think beyond classrooms and contribute to a stronger India.

01

Our Vision

To nurture socially conscious, innovative and responsible young leaders who contribute towards India's growth and development.

02

Who We Are

Think India MANIT is a student community that brings together curious minds to exchange ideas, build meaningful connections and create impact.

03

Our Goals

We aim to promote leadership, intellectual dialogue, innovation, social responsibility and opportunities for students to grow beyond academics.

WHAT WE DO
